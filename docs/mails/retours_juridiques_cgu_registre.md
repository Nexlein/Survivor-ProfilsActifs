## DECISION TRACE & SUMMARY

**1. Accepted Legal Requirements:**

- **Registre de Traitement (Data Register)**:
  - Must explicitly mention the DPO (Data Protection Officer) and the Data Controller(s) (Responsable(s) de traitement).
  - Must explicitly define Data Retention periods (durées de conservation).
  - Retention periods must be strictly minimized to comply with GDPR.
- **CGU (Terms of Service)**:
  - Fix the contradiction in Article 2 regarding user age (control vs non-mandatory).
  - Remove highly technical jargon (e.g., "bcrypt" in Article 3).
  - Rewrite and "soften" the tone to make it user-friendly and accessible to non-technical citizens.

**2. DECISION TRACE:**

- **Decision:** The legal documents (`legal_cgu.md` and `legal_data_register.md`) must be overhauled for compliance and tone.
- **Reasoning:** Mandated by the Ministry's Legal Advisor to ensure GDPR compliance and accessibility for the general public.
